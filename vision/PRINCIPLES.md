# Security Strategy Principles

1. Business first, security second.
2. Protect value, not inventory equally.
3. Think in attack paths, not isolated findings.
4. Use threat-informed prioritization.
5. Optimize controls for risk reduction, not control count.
6. Make residual risk explicit.
7. Assume compromise and design for resilience.
8. Evidence before assertion.
9. Unknowns are first-class data.
10. Decisions must be traceable.
11. Metrics must measure outcomes where possible.
12. Strategy must be reviewable, versioned, and adaptable.
13. Frameworks are coordinates, not the destination.
14. Prefer reversible experiments before irreversible commitments.
15. Keep `main` authoritative; use branches to explore futures.
